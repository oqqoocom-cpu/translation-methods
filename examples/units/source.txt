Clause 1. Purpose. This shared orchard rule of the North Orchard Council (NOC) lets member households pick fruit from common trees for their own use. Fruit taken under this rule shall not be sold.

Clause 2. Members. A member household is a household registered with the council that has joined at least one spring planting. Registration is free and may be made at any regular meeting.

Clause 3. Common trees. The NOC keeps a list of common trees and marks each one with a green band. Trees without a band belong to individual households and are not covered by this rule.

Clause 4. Picking hours. Members may pick from sunrise to sunset. Members shall not use ladders or tools that can damage branches.

Clause 5. Reporting damage. A member who finds a damaged tree shall report it to the council within 14 days. The report may be made orally or in writing.

Clause 6. Shared tools. The NOC shall store shared tools in the tool shed and lend them in the order of requests. Borrowed tools shall be returned before the next regular meeting.

Clause 7. Harvest days. Before the main harvest, the council shall announce the harvest days at a regular meeting. On those days each household picks only from the trees assigned to it.

Clause 8. Surplus fruit. Fruit left after the harvest days shall be offered first to households with young children and then to the neighbouring school.

Clause 9. Breach. A household that sells fruit in breach of Clause 1 shall pay a fine of 1,250 orchard credits and may be suspended from picking until the end of the season.

Clause 10. Appeals. A household may appeal a fine or a suspension. The NOC shall hear the appeal at the next regular meeting and give its decision in writing.

Clause 11. Records. The council shall keep minutes of every regular meeting, including the list of common trees, the harvest days and all appeal decisions.

Clause 12. Amendments. Any change to this rule requires the approval of a majority of member households at a regular meeting. The NOC shall post the amended text on the notice board.
